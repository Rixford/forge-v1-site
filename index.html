FORGE V1

Custom workouts built around the day you are actually having.

FORGE V1 helps you build a clear training session around your goal, workout style, body focus, available time, equipment, and limits. Each workout is organized into warmup, main block, optional finisher, and cooldown, with readable coaching cues and exercise detail.

What it supports

HIIT, strength, weights, boxing, running, cycling, swimming, rowing, yoga, mobility, and Pilates.

How it helps

Clear blocks, step-by-step exercise guidance, copyable workout summaries, and recent workouts saved on device.

Who it is for

Solo training, small-group coaching, or class planning when you need structure without extra clutter.
