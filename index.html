FORGE

Custom workouts, built for the way you train.

FORGE helps you create clear, structured workouts around your goal, training style, body focus, time, equipment, and limits. Choose what you want, set what you have, add anything the workout should account for, and FORGE builds a session you can actually follow.

Each workout is organized into warmup, main block, optional finisher, and cooldown, with coaching cues, work and rest details, and step-by-step exercise guidance when you need it.

Built for flexible training

FORGE supports HIIT, strength, weights, boxing, running, cycling, swimming, rowing, yoga, mobility, Pilates, bodyweight training, dumbbells, kettlebells, and more.

Simple enough to start

Use quick limits like low impact, knee friendly, no jumping, no running, small space, or quiet training. Add your own notes for injuries, energy level, class size, available equipment, or preferred style.

Use it your way

Train solo, lead a small group, plan a class, or save a workout for later. Copy the overview for a quick summary, or copy the full detail when you want every step, cue, and instruction outside the app.
