FORGE V1 for iPhone

What FORGE does

FORGE V1 builds structured workouts around your goal, training style, body focus, duration, equipment, quick limits, and optional freeform constraints. Sessions are organized into warmup, main block, optional finisher, and cooldown, with copyable overview and detailed workout output.

Contact

Support email: breatheintent@yahoo.com

Subscription management

  • Open FORGE V1 and tap the flame button in the upper-left corner of the workout builder.
  • Tap Manage Subscription to open Apple's subscription management sheet.
  • You can also manage or cancel from the App Store account subscription settings.

New eligible subscribers may see a 7-day free trial. Trial access is limited in app to 3 successful custom workouts on day 1, then 1 successful custom workout per day for days 2 through 7.

Restore purchases

If your subscription or top-up access does not appear, open the account screen from the flame button and tap Restore. FORGE uses native StoreKit purchase restoration.

Troubleshooting workout generation

  • Confirm your iPhone has a working internet connection.
  • Make sure your subscription is active, or that trial/top-up generations remain.
  • If a purchase completed but access did not refresh, use Restore first.
  • If workout generation still fails, contact support with a short description of the issue and what screen you were on.
