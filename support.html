FORGE for iPhone

What FORGE does

FORGE builds a workout from your goal, training style, body focus, duration, equipment, session use, and limits. A workout can include warmup, main work, an optional finisher, cooldown, rest, cues, and movement detail.

Contact

Support email: breatheintent@yahoo.com

Subscription management

  1. Open FORGE and choose Account in the upper-left corner of the workout builder.
  2. Choose Manage subscription to open Apple's subscription settings.
  3. You can also manage or cancel from App Store account settings.

FORGE includes 3 total free workout generations before subscription. An active monthly subscription provides 50 workout generations per billing period.

Restore purchase

Open Account and choose Restore purchases to restore an active monthly subscription purchased with this Apple ID.

Consumable top-ups cannot be restored from App Store purchase history. If a completed top-up is missing, contact support with the top-up size and approximate purchase time. Do not send your Apple ID or receipt data.

Interrupted build

If a workout build was interrupted, reopen FORGE. The app keeps the pending build and resumes it when possible. You can also choose Resume build from the builder.

Build troubleshooting

  1. Confirm the iPhone has a working internet connection.
  2. Check the generation balance in Account.
  3. If an active subscription is missing, choose Restore purchases.
  4. If the build still fails, contact support with the approximate time, selected duration, and the screen where the failure appeared.

Do not send sensitive medical information, an Apple ID, receipt data, or a session token.

Product boundary

FORGE builds workouts from the choices you enter. It is not a medical service and does not replace licensed medical advice or in-person coaching judgment.
